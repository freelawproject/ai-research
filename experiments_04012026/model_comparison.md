## Evaluation Note
All F1 metrics below are computed after downsampling the "Cited by" class to 1% of its original count (records where both predicted and label treatment are "Cited by"). "Cited by" is the overwhelmingly dominant class — most cited cases receive no negative treatment. Without downsampling, high accuracy on this majority class would inflate metrics and mask poor performance on the rarer negative treatments (Stop, Warning, Caution) that are the primary concern for a legal citator.

## Recommendation

**Sonnet 4.6** is the recommended model. It leads in completeness (99.19% match rate) and direction classification (macro F1 0.84), with the highest severity macro F1 (tied with Kimi at 0.79). While treatment macro F1 is low across all models, Sonnet is the only model that correctly identifies "Cert. denied as recognized by" (F1 = 0.47) — a treatment all other models miss entirely.

**Kimi K2.5** shows the strongest treatment macro F1 (0.46) and ties Sonnet on severity (0.79), but is held back by its low match rate (73.45%, missing 60 cases). If completeness were improved, it could be a viable cost-effective alternative at $60.49/1K records.

**Haiku 4.5** is the cheapest option ($44.17/1K) with decent completeness (95.30%) but substantially weaker classification: direction macro F1 of 0.53 (Related Reference = 0.34) and treatment macro F1 of 0.26 make it unreliable without further prompt tuning.

**Gemini 3.1 Pro** is not recommended: most expensive ($222.88/1K), lowest severity macro F1 (0.42), and only 76% match rate. **Gemini 3 Flash** failed entirely due to MAX_TOKENS limits.
