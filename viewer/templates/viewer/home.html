  • A dispute is one contiguous run of disagreeing words/symbols found when the engines' aligned normalized texts are compared — not a bbox: one block can hold several disputes, and a dispute maps back to its block(s) by token provenance.
  • Cells read numerator/denominator: 41/56 means 41 of the 56 disputes the comparison found for that combination. The denominator is always the disputes column.
  • 1st engine — the main: the reconstruction skeleton, and the reading that is kept whenever a dispute goes unresolved.
  • main bboxes — every block the 1st (main) engine detected across the pages; disputes map back to these blocks by token provenance.
  • majority — disputes a 2-of-3 majority resolved.
  • reorders — paired deletion + insertion of near-identical text: both engines carry the content but disagree on where it belongs (e.g. physical vs semantic footnote order); the main's placement is kept.
  • rejected or refused — disputes where the resolver could not vote: no cached crop read, read rejected by the ending guard, dispute not located in the read, or a refused/empty supplemental stream.
  • low conf. — disputes with no majority: the main reading was kept and flagged.
  • high risk — low-confidence disputes where the disputed span is longer than 10 units on some side.
