<author id="AysW">Hill, Justice,</author>
<p id="AS-H">concurring in part and dissenting in part.</p>
<p id="A_2c">I concur in Divisions 1, 2 (b), 3, 4 and 5 of the majority opinion. As for Division 2 (a), I agree with so much of Justice Marshall’s dissent as points out that the testator intended the Item II bequest to be funded with bonds valued at face value the same as testator intended in the Item IV and V bequests.</p>
<p id="b700-18">I am authorized to state that Presiding Justice Jordan shares <page-number citation-index="1" label="671">*671</page-number>these views.</p>
