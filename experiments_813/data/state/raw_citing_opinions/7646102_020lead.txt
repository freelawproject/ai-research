<author id="b356-20">GAUDIN, Judge.</author>
<p id="b356-21">From the record, it is apparent that judgment was rendered in open court on March 15, 1989. The minutes for that date read:</p>
<blockquote id="AvHm">“This matter came for trial on this date with all parties at interest present. After due examination the Court rendered a judgment in favor of the plaintiff and against the defendant in the amount of $641.21 plus costs of court reduced by $60.00 to correct the problem. Reeon-ventional Demand denied.”</blockquote>
<p id="b356-22">On March 27, the defendant appealed; however, no written judgment has yet been signed in conformity with LSA-C.C.P. art. 1911. Until one is signed and lodged with this Court, the appeal is premature and subject to dismissal. See <em>Overmier v. Traylor, </em><span class="citation" data-id="1772937"><a href="/opinion/1772937/overmier-v-traylor/" aria-description="Citation for case: Overmier v. Traylor">475 So.2d 1094</a></span> (La.1985); and <em>Perkins v. Salas, </em><span class="citation" data-id="7558128"><a href="/opinion/7626344/perkins-v-salas/" aria-description="Citation for case: Perkins v. Salas">507 So.2d 827</a></span> (La.App. 5 Cir.1987).</p>
<p id="b356-23">Nonetheless, this case was placed on the docket and called for oral argument on November 8, 1989, but neither appellant nor his attorney was present.</p>
<p id="b356-24">Accordingly, this appeal is hereby dismissed as being premature. Appellant is to pay costs.</p>
<p id="b356-25">APPEAL DISMISSED.</p>
</opinion>
