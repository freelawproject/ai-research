What “best case” means These figures are a ceiling, not today’s confirmed total. They count every SCOTUS→circuit link that becomes achievable once we finish the recoverable cleanup — standardizing docket numbers (including messy ones that need automated LLM cleaning), resolving lower-court names, and removing duplicates.
