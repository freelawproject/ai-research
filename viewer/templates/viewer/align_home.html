Every engine's regions are grouped onto the same pieces of the page by geometry alone — two regions link when their intersection covers at least {{ stats.overlap|default:"half" }} of the smaller one, and a group is a connected component of those links, so one engine's three boxes and another's one box become a single comparable region.

Reading order comes from the boxes' own coordinates: the columns are inferred from a gap in the left edges, and the running heads and footer are read as their own bands. The container model places nothing here — it is drawn over the page image so its output can be judged by eye.

Each region then resolves to what the engines agree on — whole where two of three match, word by word where none do. A word no two engines agree on is marked rather than silently chosen, and every region says how it landed.
